# Rig profiles

Rig authoring is deliberately outside Phase 1. Projects already carry the stable identifier
`adult_front_sitting` so prepared components can gain compatibility metadata later without a
project-format reset.

A future rig profile will version a canonical canvas and rest pose, stable slot and bone names,
pivots, layer order, required and optional slots, deformation modes, and animation-library
compatibility. Godot remains the animation-authoring authority. The goal is to animate stable bones
or slots once and switch compatible tails, eyes, ears, fur, markings, and tints at runtime.
